    4. What the numbers mean

    • Gross is pay before anything comes off. Net is what reaches your bank account after income tax, CPP (QPP in Quebec), EI and, in Quebec, QPIP.
    • Take-home here means net pay minus the cost of living there: rent, commute, other monthly costs, and one-time moving costs, plus any signing bonus after its tax.
    • Compare by per month (or per week). A 24-week term always looks bigger than a 16-week term on whole-term totals.
    • Income tax is estimated by annualising: the term's pay is scaled up to a full year, taxed with yearly brackets, then scaled back. That mirrors how payroll withholds.
    • Offers within $25/month are marked about tied: the estimates are not that precise.
